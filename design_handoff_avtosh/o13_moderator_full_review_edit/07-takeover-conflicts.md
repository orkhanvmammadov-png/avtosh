# Claim takeover & conflicts
Takeover (A saved → claim expired → B claims): amber attribution card "Bu elanda saxlanılmış moderator düzəlişi var. Saxlayan moderator: <A> · <time>. Claim artıq sizindir — bu, əvvəlki moderatorun saxlanılmış işidir." Actions: "Davam et" (primary — continue on A's saved adjustment) · "Düzəlişi sil" (secondary). Discard requires confirmation: "<A>-nın saxlanılmış düzəlişi silinəcək və tarixçədə qalacaq. Bu geri qaytarıla bilməz." Discard affects the working adjustment ONLY — never seller submission, approved listing, or public content. Never silent overwrite; previous authorship preserved in history (correction H).
Attribution model: current saved-state attribution (savedBy/savedAt) is distinct from the append-only adjustment-save history — multiple moderators across takeovers are all retained.
Stale/conflict (claim lost · newer adjustment saved elsewhere · seller resubmitted · revision counter changed · subject changed): red card "Elan başqa sessiyada dəyişdirilib. Səhifəni yeniləyin." + "Səhifəni yenilə" button. No auto-merge; decisions locked while conflict is shown; local unsaved work is not silently applied.
